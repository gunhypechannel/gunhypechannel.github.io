Delete your U.D.F. account

U.D.F. Ultra Deep Field · developer: Erik Scott

To delete your U.D.F. Ultra Deep Field account and its data:

  1. Send an email to udeepfield@gmail.com from the email address you sign in with.
  2. Use the subject Delete my U.D.F. account and include your commander name.

We delete your account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

Your sign-in account, commander name and avatar, empire and progress, resources, alliance membership, chat and private messages, feedback and notification tokens.

What may be kept

Anonymous crash and performance reports that no longer identify you may be kept for up to 12 months. Messages you posted in shared chats are deleted with your account.
