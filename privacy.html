Privacy Policy

U.D.F. Ultra Deep Field · last updated 16 November 2026

U.D.F. Ultra Deep Field ("the game") is an online space strategy game made by an independent developer. This policy explains what information the game collects, why, and the choices you have. Contact: udeepfield@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: the email address you sign in with (or your Google account's email and name if you use Google sign-in), and the commander name you choose. Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider and are never visible to us.
  • Game data: your empire, progress, resources, battles, alliance membership and settings, so your game is saved and shared with the players you play with.
  • Messages: chat messages you send in global, alliance and private chat, and feedback you send through the in-game Feedback button.
  • Diagnostics: crash and performance reports (device type, browser version, memory and frame-rate readings, game version) so we can fix crashes and lag.
  • Notifications: if you turn on notifications, a notification token for your device so we can tell you when you're attacked or an event starts.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera. The game shows no ads and does not use advertising or tracking IDs.

How we use it

Only to run the game: keeping your account and progress, showing other players your commander name, empire and messages, sending the notifications you asked for, answering feedback and fixing problems. We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not share it with anyone for advertising.

Where it is stored

Game data is stored with our hosting provider Supabase and the game is served from GitHub Pages. Data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

What other players see

Your commander name, avatar, empire, alliance, rankings and the messages you post in shared chats are visible to other players. Private messages are visible only to you and the recipient.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can ask us to delete your account and all its data at any time; see Delete your account. Crash and performance reports are kept for up to 12 months.

Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and its date.
